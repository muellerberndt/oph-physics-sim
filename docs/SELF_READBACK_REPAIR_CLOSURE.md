# Bounded atomic self-readback closure

## Purpose

This experiment asks whether a repair law can be reconstructed from the
operational evidence generated by that law without feeding the law back into
the reconstruction algorithm.

The expectation-level one-step answer is positive for one bounded integer
specialization. The result covers one oriented twelve-port carrier,
nonnegative atomic port loads, and protected totals zero through four. It
also includes exact trajectories
in the separately distinguished total-twelve sector, an exhaustive progress
audit of every nonnegative state in that sector, and a signed-state control on
all \(3^{12}\) load vectors in \(\{-1,0,1\}^{12}\). It does not establish that
the specialization is the complete repair grammar of OPH.

## One event orbit

The carrier is supplied by an explicit integer tuple of twenty cyclically
oriented faces. Its thirty undirected seams and sixty directed labels are
derived from that tuple. The experiment enumerates all 120 incidence
automorphisms and retains the sixty that preserve the oriented face cycles.
Their action on directed labels is free and transitive.

The sixty directed labels are therefore one finite torsor for the proper
rotation group. They encode the two local completion placements on each seam.
The physical attempt alphabet contains the thirty undirected seams. No
floating coordinate matching is used in this construction.

This gives a compact allocation of the proposed repair clauses:

1. A1 supplies thirty primitive undirected seam mismatch constraints.
2. A2 preserves an event's endpoint total and minimizes the integer endpoint
   disagreement. The two directions label the two nearest completions when
   parity prevents equality.
3. A3 on the one-attempt simplex assigns probability \(1/30\) to each
   indistinguishable seam. Conditional A3 on the local completion fiber
   assigns probability \(1/2\) to each unresolved odd tie.

For an event \(i\mathbin{\to}j\) with endpoint total \(s\), reconciliation is

\[
(x_i,x_j)
\longmapsto
\left(
\left\lfloor\frac{s}{2}\right\rfloor,
\left\lceil\frac{s}{2}\right\rceil
\right).
\]

When \(s\) is even, the two event orientations produce the same result. When
\(s\) is odd, they produce the two endpoint-swapped nearest-balanced results.
The transitive seam orbit gives the \(1/30\) attempt schedule. The
endpoint-swap invariant completion fiber gives the \(1/2\) odd-remainder
split.
A common continuous-time event rate remains a clock convention. A lazy
identity mixture is not silently identified with the same discrete one-tick
channel; the receipt compares positive generator rays.

## Noncircular readback

The experiment separates three data types.

`CandidateLaw` is private producer state. It contains the update policy,
event support, schedule weights, and clock.

`public_constraint_readback` exhaustively exercises the candidate on every
declared state and event exposed by the harness. It emits only operational
constraint facts:

- total preservation;
- endpoint-local writes;
- nearest-shell landing;
- reversible typing of mismatch-neutral changes;
- presentation and endpoint-reversal covariance;
- exact directed-seam support;
- full event support and uniformity;
- completeness of the state-event probe.

The reconstruction function has an exact field allowlist. It refuses
candidate identifiers, policy names, transition probabilities, and held-out
transition digests. It also refuses to use the candidate's observed support
or schedule to construct the law. The source manifest fixes the A1 seam
grammar, the local audit checks the A2 completion semantics, and A3 fixes
counting measure on the directed-seam torsor. A biased schedule or a
wrong-radius support reaches held-out scoring and fails there.

The candidate transition kernel is retained separately. Scoring occurs only
after reconstruction. With \(P\) the transition kernel, the comparison tests

\[
P_{\mathrm{candidate}}-I
=c\left(P_{\mathrm{reconstructed}}-I\right)
\]

entry by entry over the rationals for one \(c>0\). This identifies one common
continuous-time generator-rate rescaling and nothing else. The discrete
one-tick kernels are not equated. After defining
\(Q=P-I\), the corresponding continuous-time channels are \(e^{tQ}\), and a
positive multiple of \(Q\) is absorbed by rescaling \(t\).

## Exhaustive bounded result

Every weak composition of totals zero through four is probed. The state and
terminal-shell counts are:

| Total | States | Terminal shell |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 1 | 1 |
| 1 | 12 | 12 |
| 2 | 78 | 66 |
| 3 | 364 | 220 |
| 4 | 1,365 | 495 |

For every tested total:

- every positive-probability transition preserves the total and does not
  increase the integer quadratic energy;
- there is one closed communicating class;
- that class is exactly the nearest-balanced shell;
- every state reaches it;
- the restricted kernel is exactly doubly stochastic;
- its unique stationary state is uniform on the shell.

The one-atom sector gives the exact mean identity

\[
P_1=I-\frac1{60}L_{\mathrm{ico}}.
\]

The same conditional-mean identity is checked on all 1,820 states in the
exhaustive total-zero-through-four probe:

\[
\mathbb E[X_{n+1}\mid X_n=x]
=
\left(I-\frac1{60}L_{\mathrm{ico}}\right)x.
\]

The full integer kernel remains nonlinear. The identity connects its first
moment to the scalar Laplacian repair certificate. It does not identify
repair with the unitary response channel. The factor \(i\), reversible
propagation, and physical time do not follow from it.

Uniform one-attempt frequencies do not establish an independent schedule.
The receipt therefore evaluates all 900 ordered seam-attempt pairs on the
one-particle sector, with the local tie sampled conditionally. The IID law
gives \(P^2\). A correlated control that repeats its first seam has the same
\(1/30\) attempt marginal and a different two-step kernel. This is a
countermodel to deriving Markovity from canonical A3.

The proposed repair amendment can close this gap conditionally. A1-R must
prove that all finite words in the primitive attempt alphabet are admissible,
with counting measure fixed before candidate dynamics is exposed. An abstract
tick is a seam readback and reconciliation attempt. All thirty attempts remain
enabled at every state, including attempts whose completion is a wait. A2-R
must prove that the temporal constraint grammar is complete and contains no
state-dependent deletion or cross-position coupling. A3 on the resulting
word space then gives \(30^{-k}\) to every length-\(k\) attempt word. These
finite laws are prefix-consistent and define the IID process. Conditioning on strict
or committed repairs would produce a different, state-dependent schedule.
The receipt certifies the conditional factorization theorem and keeps the
canonical-A3 coupled-closure receipt false until those clauses are adopted and
discharged.

## Total twelve

The local source projection pins the integer counting packet's twelve atomic
writes at the first port, its upstream hashes, and the quadratic cost
\(H(x)=\sum_i x_i^2\). The simulator campaign closes the nonnegative
total-twelve sector containing that full-pile state. Integer convexity gives
the unique minimum

\[
(1,1,\ldots,1),
\qquad H_{\min}=12,
\]

with next energy floor fourteen. The nonnegative sector contains
\(\binom{23}{11}=1,352,078\) states. Every one of them is inspected.

For each nonminimum state, the audit constructs a path of at most three
directed events. Unit-difference swaps move an extreme along a shortest
carrier path, after which complete balancing strictly lowers \(H\). Exactly
1,352,077 states have such a path and the all-one state is the sole minimum.
Full event support and finiteness then leave one closed class. The former
one-unit law has 303 absorbing states in the same sector, of which 302 are
false local minima.

The completed nearest-balancing event is a macro-event that can move several
units in one step. It is not the counting packet's atomic one-unit record
move. The exact local terminal-shell bridge is known; an instrument-level
macro-event-to-record-history theorem remains open.

Five exact integer trajectories separately exercise long schedules from
concentrated, split, lopsided, alternating, and four-block states. A fixed
integer event stream selects only directed-seam atoms. Every trajectory
settles at the all-one state.

For a general total \(S=12q+r\), minimum-energy loads lie in
\(\{q,q+1\}\), with exactly \(r\) upper loads. On that shell, the event law is
the lazy symmetric \(r\)-token exclusion process on the connected carrier.
Lean proves the local sector preservation, residual parity, exact rational
mean, quadratic-drop identity, and strict local descent.

The same progress construction works for signed loads. From a maximum port,
take a shortest path to a port at least two lower. If no seam has an immediate
gap of two, directed unit-difference events transport the maximum along that
path without changing the energy; the final event lowers it strictly. The
carrier diameter bounds the word length by three. Nearest balancing preserves
the initial coordinate range, so the reachable fixed-total set is finite.
IID full support therefore reaches the balanced shell almost surely. The
signed-cube audit checks this construction on 531,441 states. A fully
formalized global hitting theorem remains a separate proof target.

## Adversarial controls

The frozen suite includes:

- three clock rates for both directed-event conventions;
- a nearest-shell policy that keeps the originally high endpoint high;
- the nonlinear one-unit descent rule;
- identity and endpoint swap;
- uniform redistribution over every conserved split;
- a port-label-dependent remainder rule;
- edge-biased and orientation-biased schedules;
- distance-two, antipodal, and mixed-radius event supports.

The two globally reversed direction conventions, at the three tested clock
rates, have one normalized state-generator-ray digest after event labels are
marginalized. Their joint event-and-state histories remain distinguishable
unless A1 declares global arrow reversal a presentation convention and
supplies the corresponding quotient.

The nearest-shell high-side rule is especially important. Its public
constraints are sufficient for reconstruction, yet its held-out dynamics
does not match the reconstructed generator. This demonstrates that
reconstruction does not merely return every eligible input.

The one-unit rule has a different failure. Every balanced-shell state is
absorbing. It therefore has 12, 66, 220, and 495 absorbing states in the
positive tested sectors instead of one mixing class. Its low disagreement
does not supply coupled state-generator uniqueness.

## Event typing

An outcome that lowers mismatch belongs to `STRICT_REPAIR`. A mismatch-neutral
swap across a unit difference is reversible propagation or a gauge stutter.
An unchanged outcome is a wait and produces no committed repair. Persistent
evidence is appended through a separate record transition.

This distinction matches the transactional repair contract, which correctly
refuses to label a mismatch-neutral swap as strict descent.

## Claim boundary

The receipt establishes a bounded expectation-level one-step atomic
generator-ray fixed point within its frozen adversarial suite. The exact real
pair-average channel is the A2 equalizer retraction. The odd-total integer
process is a microhistory lift whose two directions recover that channel in
expectation. The all-word IID law is a conditional theorem under the proposed
free-composition and temporal-completeness clauses.

It does not establish:

- completeness of directed seams for the full accessible algebra;
- derivation of the integer load grammar from A1 alone;
- selection of a protected total sector;
- noncommutative conditional expectations;
- refinement compatibility;
- a continuum semigroup;
- a physical clock or physical repair law;
- Markovity from the canonical A3 statement;
- particle, gravity, or cosmological observables;
- full self-reading universe closure.

The implementation is in
`oph_fpe/dynamics/self_readback_repair_closure.py`. Focused checks are in
`tests/test_self_readback_repair_closure.py`. The byte-exact stored receipt is
`data/repair_closure/bounded_atomic_self_readback_closure_receipt.json`.
