# Canonical repair law candidate

## Question

The three OPH axioms fix an observer-screen architecture, agreement of
interpreted data, and a conditional maximum-randomness rule. The axiom
reference leaves the repair policy open. This document states a proposed
strengthening of A1 and A2 under which a finite repair policy can become a
theorem without adding a fourth axiom. The proposal is a substantive basis
amendment. It is not a consequence of the current wording and has not been
adopted.

The target is a dynamics class determined up to one common clock rate. The
clock rate does not affect the finite fixed algebra.

## Proposed allocation among the axioms

### A1: complete primitive repair grammar

At each finite regulator, A1 would supply:

1. a common compatible-section repair algebra or module on which every
   primitive move acts;
2. the complete finite list of primitive repair locations;
3. each move's local equalizer and its lifted endomorphism on that common
   object;
4. the repairable working variables at each location;
5. the protected subalgebra that every repair fixes;
6. the protected conserved functionals;
7. the external append-only audit and checkpoint continuation instrument;
8. the quotient-visible base-measure data used to construct the A3
   reference; and
9. presentation and refinement maps for all of these objects.

Every accepted repair is generated by these primitive repairs and their
declared compositions. No hidden move type, label-dependent priority, fitted
coefficient, or downstream physical target is admissible.

For the scalar twelve-port specialization, the primitive locations are the
thirty undirected seams. A seam repair may change its two endpoint readings,
must preserve the endpoint-sum functional, and leaves every other working
reading unchanged. These scalar coordinates are not committed records. The
certificate does not construct an append-only checkpoint instrument.

### A2: canonical semantic reconciliation

An accepted primitive repair would be a local retraction onto the
corresponding agreement equalizer. Its lift to the common repair object fixes
the protected subalgebra, preserves every declared protected functional,
commutes with the external checkpoint diagrams, fixes accepted meaning, and
introduces no directional preference between the two sides of a seam.

In a finite algebraic formulation, the intended map is the
reference-preserving conditional expectation onto the agreement subalgebra.
The proposal is restricted to a faithful finite-dimensional reference state.
Its recognition conditions are locality to the agreement equalizer, equality
of rows inside a finite classical fiber, and detailed balance with the
faithful reference. The finite conditional-resampling recognition theorem in
`reverse-engineering-reality/Lean/ObservableNormalForms/` proves uniqueness
once those conditions and the complete fiber are supplied. Applying that
theorem to repair also requires a proof that the enumerated fiber is exactly
the set of admissible consistent completions for the protected context.

### A3: remove residual scheduling bias

A3 applies to the fixed finite simplex of primitive moves. The exact
combinatorial certificate constructs sixty directed completions from the
thirty A1 seams and proves that the proper carrier group acts simply
transitively on them. Presentation invariance and normalization therefore
leave one probability distribution: \(1/60\) per directed completion,
equivalently \(1/30\) per seam and \(1/2\) for the unresolved odd-total tie.
No adjustable uniform scalar schedule remains. Applying conditional maximum
randomness to event strings gives the IID extension only if A1-R proves free
composition of reconciliation attempts and A2-R proves temporal-constraint
completeness with no coupling. Canonical A3 alone does not imply Markovity.
The alphabet counts every attempted seam readback, including attempts that
complete as waits.

A3 does not invent the move grammar or the reconciliation map. It chooses
inside the A1-fixed move space after A2 has fixed what each move means.

## Exact scalar theorem

Let \(e=\{i,j\}\) be one seam and let
\(b_e=e_i-e_j\). A linear repair on real port readings that

1. changes only \(i\) and \(j\);
2. preserves \(x_i+x_j\);
3. is invariant under swapping \(i\) and \(j\); and
4. retracts onto \(x_i=x_j\)

is uniquely

\[
E_e=I-\frac12 b_e b_e^{\mathsf T}.
\]

It replaces the two endpoint readings by their mean. It is positive, unital,
idempotent, doubly stochastic, and preserves the total port reading.

Uniform scheduling on the thirty seams gives

\[
T=\frac1{30}\sum_e E_e
  =I-\frac1{60}L_{\mathrm{ico}},
\qquad
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{rep}}=T-I
  =-\frac1{60}L_{\mathrm{ico}}.
\]

The positive repair generator \(I-T\) has spectrum

\[
0,\quad
\frac{5-\sqrt5}{60},\quad
\frac1{10},\quad
\frac{5+\sqrt5}{60}
\]

with multiplicities \(1,3,5,3\). A common attempt rate multiplies all three
positive values and therefore leaves every dimensionless spectral ratio
fixed.

Each seam step lowers the endpoint quadratic disagreement by
\((x_i-x_j)^2/2\). The averaged dynamics converges to the constant mode on a
connected carrier.

The directed-seam certificate enumerates all 120 incidence automorphisms
without coordinates, separates the 60 proper maps by exact cyclic-face
orientation, and checks all 3,600 directed-seam transporters. The proper
subgroup acts freely and transitively on the directed completions. Its
verifier is a deterministic replay of the producer implementation rather
than an independent implementation.

## Atomic integer lift

Exact averaging of two integer registers is available when their sum is even.
For an odd sum, no exactly equal integer pair preserves the total. The
nearest-balanced shell contains the two ordered states

\[
\left(\left\lfloor\frac{s}{2}\right\rfloor,
      \left\lceil\frac{s}{2}\right\rceil\right),
\qquad
\left(\left\lceil\frac{s}{2}\right\rceil,
      \left\lfloor\frac{s}{2}\right\rfloor\right).
\]

The swap-invariant distribution assigns probability \(1/2\) to each. Its
expectation is the real pair average and its event-level residual remains
explicit. This is an exact expectation-level lift, not an exact A2
reconciliation for odd totals.

The directed completion law supplies the missing operational interpretation:
a unit-difference event is a wait in one direction and a reversible swap in
the other. The former one-unit rule suppressed that neutral motion and
terminated at false local minima. On the full nonnegative total-twelve fiber,
the one-unit rule has 303 absorbing states, while the directed law has the
all-one state as its unique closed class. An exact constructive audit checks
all 1,352,078 states and finds a path of at most three directed events from
every nonminimum state to a lower quadratic level.

This is a microhistory and expectation-level bridge. On an odd endpoint
total, neither pathwise placement lies in the exact equality equalizer. The
completed macro-event can move several units and is not the atomic one-unit
record move. Those facts keep the integer process separate from the exact real
A2 conditional expectation and from the record-writing instrument.

## What the finite result decides

The result fixes the one-step operator inside the scalar, seam-local,
complete-reconciliation grammar. The simply transitive event orbit fixes the

No theorem identifies this working-reading operator with the gauge-response

## Fixed-point interpretation

For lifted reference-preserving conditional expectations
\(\{\widehat E_a:\mathcal X_r\to\mathcal X_r\}\) on one common finite repair
object, define

\[
R_r=\sum_a w_a\widehat E_a,\qquad
K_r=I-R_r.
\]

The kernel of the positive generator is the intersection of the equalizers
whose weights are positive:

\[
\ker(I-R_r)
=\bigcap_{a:w_a>0}\operatorname{Ran}\widehat E_a.
\]

Equality with the complete joint agreement algebra requires a fair,
full-support schedule. Observable convergence to a fixed algebra does not
prove uniqueness of a stationary state on the dual space. That requires a
separate fixed-state or ergodicity theorem.

Repair dynamics relaxes toward the A3-selected state within a protected
sector. Selection of the full physical universe additionally requires the A1
state and move grammar to be complete, the A2 equalizers to cover every shared
meaning constraint, a unique coupled state-generator solution, and
cross-refinement compatibility.

The corresponding universe-level target is a self-readback equation for the
entire quotient-visible finite model in a fixed protected-record sector:

\[
\mathfrak X_r=(\mathfrak N_r,\omega_r,K_r)
\simeq
\operatorname{Reconstruct}_{r,p}
\left(\operatorname{PublicReadback}_{r,p}(\mathfrak X_r)\right).
\]

The public-readback map goes from the quotient-visible model category to a
separately typed evidence space. The reconstruction map returns a
quotient-visible model and must be fixed source-side before its solutions or
laboratory data are inspected. The equivalence permits declared presentation
and gauge changes and one common clock rescaling, but does not identify
different protected sectors. A unique equivalence class across the complete
A1 model class, compatible through refinement, would supply the desired
dynamical selection principle without asking A3 to choose between unrelated
ontology classes. Unconditional selection would additionally require a
source-derived sector choice. No such reconstruction or uniqueness theorem is
certified here.

The reference-preserving expectations depend on the A3-selected state. That
is noncircular when the state is computed from a target-free A1 and A2 grammar
before the channel is built. The channel then realizes that state rather than
adding physical information absent from the grammar. A stronger
self-referential claim requires a separate existence-and-uniqueness theorem
for the coupled state and generator equations. The finite scalar certificate
does not prove that global result. The exact bounded reconstruction,
adversarial controls, two-event schedule check, and total-twelve audit are
documented in `docs/SELF_READBACK_REPAIR_CLOSURE.md`.

## Promotion gates

The canonical repair law is promoted from a finite candidate only after all
of the following checks pass:

1. complete primitive move grammar on the full accessible algebra;
2. complete typing of repairable data, protected subalgebras, conserved
   functionals, and external checkpoint instruments;
3. existence and uniqueness of every reference-preserving conditional
   expectation;
4. optimizer-to-transition implementation;
5. presentation covariance for every primitive move;
6. full support, termination, or asymptotic convergence under the selected
   fair schedule;
7. refinement naturality or a proved renormalized intertwiner;
8. a separate dual fixed-state theorem modulo declared protected sectors;
9. uniqueness of the coupled state-generator fixed point;
10. source-defined full self-readback reconstruction and uniqueness across
    the admissible A1 model class; and
11. source-to-physical-observable attachment for any empirical claim.

Writing \(\Delta_r\) for the raw graph Laplacian, midpoint refinement obeys

\[
Q\Delta_fJ=\frac12\Delta_c.
\]

The per-total-attempt positive generator is
\(K_r=\Delta_r/(2|E_r|)\). Since midpoint refinement quadruples the number of
seams,

\[
QK_fJ=\frac18K_c.
\]

The strong generator and second-order semigroup intertwiners fail. A
renormalized refinement theorem and a source-derived cross-regulator clock
remain necessary.

There is a sharper state-level boundary at the first refinement. Let \(J\)
average coarse endpoint values onto the thirty edge midpoints, and let \(Q\)
restrict a fine field to the twelve inherited vertices. For the level-one
Laplacian, the exact observability matrix

\[
\mathcal O=(Q,\,Q\Delta_f,\,\ldots,\,Q\Delta_f^{41})
\]

has rank \(29\) over \(\mathbb Q\). Its kernel therefore has dimension \(13\). An
explicit six-midpoint vector \(v\) in this kernel gives two positive fields,
\(J\mathbf 1\) and \(J\mathbf 1+v/2\), with the same coarse field and the same
inherited-vertex trace under the complete averaged repair semigroup. Their
centered degree-six Legendre pair powers are \(0\) and \(15/57344\). The
proper-rotation orbit of the second field has constant ensemble mean and a
nonzero invariant covariance.

This calculation concerns the uniform-schedule expectation semigroup.
Individual seam microhistories need not agree. It also uses inherited-vertex
restriction as a declared finite readout scaffold, without identifying that
restriction or the unit-counting measure with a physical sky measurement.
The IID schedule is a declared branch whose Markov extension is not implied
by bare A3. The missing source object is a
refinement-detail state/readout instrument that selects the detail-fiber
probability law and supplies the physical output binding.

No fourth axiom is warranted while these derivation gates are unresolved.
If two inequivalent repair laws survive after the complete A1 grammar, A2
reconciliation conditions, and A3 reference are fixed, the surviving choice
would be a genuine additional physical premise.
